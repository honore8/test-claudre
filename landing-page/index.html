The Salon
Convened by Bluemind Foundation. Shaped by Africa. Open to the World.

A cultural gathering where evidence, public leadership and the wisdom of communities come together to shape the future of care.

The Three Acts

Act I — The Evidence

Trust as Health Infrastructure

World premiere of the first evidence demonstrating how trusted community spaces can become mental health infrastructure.

Act II — From Evidence to Infrastructure

How do we build mental health systems people already trust?

A conversation between government, philanthropy, science and community leaders on what it takes to move from evidence to national scale.

Act III — La Clairière

A Celebration of Black Cultures of Care

An immersive evening of music, food, conversation and style celebrating hair as memory, entrepreneurship, identity and one of humanity's oldest infrastructures of trust.
