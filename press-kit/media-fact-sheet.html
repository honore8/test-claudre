THE SALON

What

An inaugural, invitation-only cultural gathering of approximately 50 guests, convened by Bluemind Foundation.

When

September 17, 2026

Where

Praxis' New York headquarters, during the 81st UN General Assembly (UNGA).

Who

By invitation only — approximately 50 guests.

Structure

  • Act I — The Evidence. Seated presentations, lower level.
  • Act II — From Evidence to Infrastructure. Seated presentations, lower level.
  • Act III — La Clairière. A standing celebration of Black cultures of care — food, music, conversation — upper level.
