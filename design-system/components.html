Buttons

Hairline text buttons

No filled or saturated buttons, no shadows, no rounded pill shapes. A button is a bordered word.

Request an Invitation Learn More

When to useOn white or ivory surfaces. Default button style.

<a href="#" class="btn">Request an Invitation</a>
Request an Invitation Learn More

When to useOn a Bluemind Blue field — inverts the hairline and label to white.

<a href="#" class="btn btn--on-blue">Request an Invitation</a>
Titles

Display headline treatments

THE SALON

When to use.title--xl — cover and poster-scale moments, one per surface.

<p class="title title--xl">THE SALON</p>

An Evening of Craft & Continuity

When to use.title--l — page and act-level headlines.

<p class="title title--l">An Evening of Craft &amp; Continuity</p>
Section Headers

Eyebrow + headline pairing

Act II — The Table

Conversation, Seated

When to useOpening any act, page section, or programme segment. The eyebrow always carries the categorization (act number, date, location); the headline carries the idea.

<div class="section-header">
  <span class="eyebrow">Act II — The Table</span>
  <h3 class="section-header-title">Conversation, Seated</h3>
</div>
Quotes

Pull-quotes

Founding Note, THE SALON

  <p>"The room is small on purpose..."</p>

</blockquote>

Forms

Hairline fields, no pills, no shadows

When to useInvitation-request and RSVP forms. Labels always use .eyebrow; fields are hairline underlines/borders, never rounded pills, never shadowed.

<div class="form-field">
  <label class="eyebrow" for="c-name">Full Name</label>
  <input class="input" type="text" id="c-name">
</div>

<label class="form-check">
  <input type="checkbox"> I will attend in person
</label>
